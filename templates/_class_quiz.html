    Stuck?

    Can't drag the button? Show your bookmarks bar first (Ctrl Shift B; in Safari, View → Show Favorites Bar), then drag it again. In Firefox you can also right-click it and choose Bookmark Link. Or use “Copy and paste instead”.

    Clicked it in Canvas and nothing happened? Look for a small Send to Scheduler box at the top right of Canvas. No box? Make sure you're signed in to Canvas, then click the button again.

    Your course isn't in the list? Open the course in Canvas, then click Send to Scheduler there.

    No email addresses came over? Some schools hide them from instructors. Students then sign in with a PIN instead of an emailed code, which works fine.

    Students missing? Canvas can take a day or two to list new enrollments, and an unpublished course may show none.

    Can't get the list out of Canvas at all? Copy the table on your course's People page and paste it under (names only; students sign in with a PIN).

    Downloaded a file? Delete it afterwards: it holds grades and more that this site doesn't keep.
