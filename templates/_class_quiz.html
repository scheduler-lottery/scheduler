    or in a new tab

    1. Sign in, then click your course. If Canvas asks, sign in. You'll land on your Dashboard: click the card for this class.

    2. Click Course Analytics in the course's menu on the left. (Some schools call it New Analytics. No menu? Click the three-line menu button at the top left.)

    3. Click the Students tab, then the download button: an arrow pointing down, next to Chart Options. A file downloads, students.csv or a .zip. Either one works.

    4. Drag that file into the box below, or click the box and choose it from your Downloads folder.

    Faster next time: your course's address

    On any page of your course in Canvas, copy the address from the top of the browser and paste it here. Then the Canvas button opens your course directly.

    We'll remember it on this computer for this sheet.

    Have an AI assistant do it

    An AI assistant that can use your web browser (Claude in Chrome, for example) can do these steps for you, signed in as you. Copy these instructions and give them to it:

    Stuck?

    No download button? In Course Analytics, open the Reports tab and click Run Report next to Class Roster. That file works too.

    No Course Analytics in the course menu? Go to Settings → Navigation, drag it into the visible list, and click Save.

    Can't download the list at all? Copy it from your course's People page instead: select the whole table, copy it, and paste it under .

    Students missing? The course has to be published, and Canvas can take a day or two to add new enrollments.

    Afterwards, delete the downloaded file: it holds grades and more that this site doesn't keep.
