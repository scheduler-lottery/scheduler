How ranking works

You rank the days, favorite first. When sign-ups close, you get the highest day on your list that still has a seat.

Step by step

  1. 1 Rank every day Favorite first, down to your last choice.
  2. 2 Sign-ups close At the deadline, or when your instructor closes them.
  3. 3 Ties get a fair draw When too many want a day, a fixed random draw decides.
  4. 4 You get your day The highest one on your list with a seat left.

Good to know

  • Rank in your true order. No other order can get you a better day.
  • Ranking early gives no advantage: the time you rank is never used.
  • Mark a day you can't make only for a real conflict. You'll never be given a day you marked.
  • Each class has one fixed draw, so the same rankings always give the same schedule. Nobody can redraw for a better result.
    • {% if instructor and not nav_student %}
  • As the instructor, you choose how ties are broken and can move students by hand afterwards. Every change is kept in the sheet's history.
