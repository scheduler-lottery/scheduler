Why does the order matter?

Each day has a limited number of places. After everyone ranks, the site gives each student a day as high on their own list as it can. If more students want a day than it has places, a fair random draw decides.

Put your real favorite first. Ranking a day higher than you really want it never helps you, and ranking early doesn't help either — when you sent your ranking isn't used at all.

“I really can't do this day” is for real conflicts. You won't be put on a day you marked — ever. If you mark every day, or every day you can do fills up, your instructor decides where you go — nobody is bumped automatically. Notes are seen only by your instructor.
