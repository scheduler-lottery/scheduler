Get your class list in one click

Your students' names and email addresses come right here, to check before anything is added. If Canvas wants you to sign in, a Canvas tab opens for that, and this page carries on after.

First, add the free Scheduler Helper to Chrome. You only do this once.

Add Scheduler Helper to Chrome
  1. In the tab that opens, click Add to Chrome.
  2. Click Add extension.
  3. Come back to this tab. Your class list is next.
  1. In the tab that opens, click Allow extensions from other stores at the top, then Allow.
  2. Click Get, then Add extension.
  3. Come back to this tab. Your class list is next.
  1. In the App Store, click Get, then Install, then Open.
  2. Click the button that opens Safari's settings, and tick Scheduler Helper.
  3. On the Scheduler Helper page that opens, click Allow. Then come back to this tab and reload it.

Or upload Canvas's student file instead

  1. Sign in, then click your course. If Canvas asks, sign in as usual. On your Dashboard, click this class's card.

  2. Click Course Analytics in the course's menu on the left.

  3. Click Students, then the download button (the arrow pointing down, next to Chart Options). A file downloads.

  4. {% if guide_download is not defined or guide_download %}

    Drag that file into the box below, or click the box and choose it from your Downloads folder.

    {% else %}

    Drag that file into the box under “Replace it with a new file”, below. You'll see what changes before anything is replaced.

    {% endif %}
