Scheduler Helper is a small, free add-on for Chrome (an “extension”) that copies your class list from Canvas into this page when you ask. You add it once on this computer. After that, one button here brings your class list over.

Or use Canvas's file of your students instead

This part is easiest on a computer. Everything so far is saved, so you can stop here: on your computer, sign in to {{ app_name }}, click My sheets, then “{{ sheet.title }}”{% if has_list %}, then Class changed in Canvas? Update from Canvas{% else %}, and add your students from there{% endif %}. Or, on this phone, {% if has_list %}add anyone new in the Add students box above{% else %} instead{% endif %}.

Canvas can give you a file listing your students. You save it from Canvas (steps 1 to 3), then choose it here (step 4). It also has grades in it: {{ app_name }} keeps only the names and email addresses, and throws everything else away as it reads the file, never saving it.

Canvas opens in a new tab, next to this one at the top of the window. These steps stay here.

Canvas opens on top of this page, and this page stays open underneath. Once Canvas has saved its file, come back here: open your browser's list of open pages, and tap this one. Can't find it? Open {{ app_name }} again: you're still signed in, and this sheet is under My sheets.

  1. Click this class's card on your Canvas Dashboard. No card for this class? Click Courses in the column of icons down the far left side of Canvas, then the class's name (still not there? click All Courses at the top of that list). If Canvas asks you to sign in first, sign in there as usual: {{ app_name }} never sees your password.

  2. Click Course Analytics in the course's menu on the left (at some schools it's called New Analytics). On a phone, that menu is hidden: tap your course's name at the top of Canvas to open it.

  3. Click Students, then the download button (the arrow pointing down, next to Chart Options). Canvas saves one file in your Downloads folder; its name ends in .zip; Windows shows it as a zipped folder. You don't need to open it. If Safari asks whether to allow downloads from Canvas, click Allow. Safari then opens Canvas's file into a folder in your Downloads. That's fine: your list is inside. Your browser shows it at the top right of its window once it's saved. Then come back to this page: click “{{ sheet.title }}” at the top of the window. If your phone asks whether to download it, tap Download.
