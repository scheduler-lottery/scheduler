The easy way: one button

Click Send to Scheduler in your bookmarks bar. Canvas opens in a new tab: click it again there{% if sheet.canvas_course %}, on {{ sheet.canvas_course }}{% else %} and pick this class{% endif %}. You'll come right back here with the list. (In Safari, clicking it here does nothing: press Open Canvas, then click Send to Scheduler in Canvas.)

  1. Find your bookmarks bar: a thin row of bookmarks just under the address bar. Not there? Press Ctrl Shift B (or, in Safari, choose View → Show Favorites Bar). If a bar you had disappears, it was already showing: press the same keys again.

  2. Drag this button up onto that bar. Hold the mouse button down on it, move it up, then let go. You only do this once.

    Send to Scheduler

  3. Click it there, in the bookmarks bar, now. This page says “It works”, and Canvas opens in a new tab. (Safari skips this check: press Open Canvas instead.)

  4. In Canvas, click Send to Scheduler again{% if not sheet.canvas_course %} and pick this class{% endif %}. Sign in first if Canvas asks. You'll come right back here with the list.

The button reads only your students' names and email addresses, and changes nothing in Canvas. A small Send to Scheduler box shows at the top right of Canvas while it works.

Rather not add a bookmark? Copy and paste instead
  1. Canvas opens in a window beside this one. Signed out of Canvas? , then press Show my Canvas courses again.
  2. Click inside that window, press Ctrl A to select everything, then Ctrl C to copy it. It looks like computer code. That's normal: it's the list Canvas itself uses.
  3. In that window, click Copy at the top (Firefox's own button).

Only names and email addresses are kept; the rest of what you copy stays on this computer. Best not done while sharing your screen.

Or download a file from Canvas

  1. Sign in, then click your course. If Canvas asks, sign in. You'll land on your Dashboard: click the card for this class.

  2. Click Course Analytics in the course's menu on the left. (Some schools call it New Analytics. No menu? Click the three-line menu button at the top left.)

  3. Click the Students tab, then the download button: an arrow pointing down, next to Chart Options. A file downloads, students.csv or a .zip. Either one works.

  4. Drag that file into the box below, or click the box and choose it from your Downloads folder.

No download button? In Course Analytics, open Reports and click Run Report next to Class Roster: that file works too. Course Analytics can be a day or two behind.

Faster next time: your course's address

On any page of your course in Canvas, copy the address from the top of the browser and paste it here. Then “Open Canvas beside this page” goes straight to your course.

We'll remember it on this computer for this sheet.
