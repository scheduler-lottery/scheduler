Your class list stays private.

  • Only names and emails are kept. Grades, Canvas IDs and every other column are thrown away the moment the file is read, before anything is saved. After an upload, you'll see exactly which columns were discarded.
  • Only you can see it. No other instructor can open your sheet, and the site has no page that shows your list to anyone else. Emails are used only to send sign-in codes; students never see them in full.
  • Encrypted all the way. From your browser to this site (HTTPS), from the site to its database (TLS), and on the database's disks (AES-256).
  • Gone when you say so. Delete a sheet and everything in it, its backups included, is erased for good after 30 days (the wait lets you undo a mistake).

You don't have to take our word for it: the site's code is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does. More in the privacy details.
