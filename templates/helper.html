What it does

  • On a sign-up sheet's Your students step (or, later, under Class changed in Canvas? on its class page), you click Get my class list from Canvas.
  • The helper asks your school's Canvas for your list of courses, then for the students in one course: the one your sheet is named for, or, if that isn't clear, the one you pick. It does that as you, signed in to Canvas the usual way. If you aren't signed in, Canvas's sign-in page comes up for you to sign in (with Duo, as always), and you're brought straight back.
  • It hands {{ app_name }}'s page each student's name and email address, and nothing else. You look the list over, and press Add.

What it never does

  • It never sees your password, and never changes anything in Canvas.
  • It does nothing until you click that button on {{ app_name }}'s own site. No other site can ask it for anything.
  • It keeps nothing, and sends nothing anywhere but this site.

What your browser will tell you

When you add it, Chrome or Edge says it can “read and change your data on canvas.northwestern.edu”. That's the permission it needs to ask Canvas for your class list; it only ever reads your list of courses and the names and emails of one course's students, and only when you click. Its code is public: see exactly what it does.

Removing it

Once your class list is in, {{ app_name }} offers to remove it for you: press Remove Scheduler Helper from Chrome. Or, any time, click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the three dots next to Scheduler Helper, then Remove from Chrome. Without it, you can still use Canvas's file of your students (the Your students step shows where to click), or add the helper again later.
