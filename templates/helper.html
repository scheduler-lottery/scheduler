Scheduler Helper

A small browser extension that brings your class list from Canvas into {{ app_name }} with one click, so there's nothing to download, copy, or drag.

What it does

  • On a sign-up sheet's Class list step, you click Get my class list from Canvas.
  • The helper asks your school's Canvas for your list of courses, then for the students in the one you pick. It does that as you, signed in to Canvas the usual way. If you aren't signed in, a Canvas tab opens for you to sign in (with Duo, as always), and it carries on after.
  • It hands {{ app_name }}'s page each student's name and email address, and nothing else. You look the list over, and press Add.

What it never does

  • It never sees your password, and never changes anything in Canvas.
  • It does nothing until you click that button on {{ app_name }}'s own site. No other site can ask it for anything.
  • It keeps nothing, and sends nothing anywhere but this site.

What your browser will tell you

When you add it, Chrome or Edge says it can “read and change your data on canvas.northwestern.edu”. That's the permission it needs to ask Canvas for your class list; it only ever reads your list of courses and the names and emails of that course's students, and only when you click. Safari asks you to allow it on Canvas and on {{ app_name }}: a Scheduler Helper page with an Allow button does that in one click. Its code is public: see exactly what it does.

Removing it

In Chrome or Edge, open the puzzle-piece menu at the top right, find Scheduler Helper, and choose Remove. In Safari, open Settings, then Extensions, and untick it (or delete the Scheduler Helper app). Without it, you can still upload Canvas's student file: the Class list step shows where to click.
