Let Scheduler Helper read your class list from Canvas?

Your school's Canvas:

Press Allow, then Allow again in the box Chrome shows. You do this once for your school; then your class list comes straight back to Scheduler.

What it reads, and what it keeps
  • It reads only your list of courses and, for the one you pick, the students' names and email addresses. It changes nothing in Canvas and never sees your password.
  • It keeps nothing: not the class list, not the course, not your Canvas sign-in. It hands the names and emails to Scheduler's page and forgets them.

Its code is public: github.com/scheduler-lottery/scheduler
